This MAE 2250 project focused on designing a practical way to separate spotted lanternflies from grapes during mechanical harvesting. Our team developed, tested, and refined a density-based separation concept that could fit into existing vineyard workflows while protecting grape quality and improving yield.

## Project Context

Our team, LanternBye, worked with stakeholders connected to vineyard operations to explore how spotted lanternflies could be removed from harvested grapes before processing. We focused on a liquid-based density separator that lets grapes sink while SLF float and can be collected separately.

Across the semester, the project moved through three key phases:

- defining the client problem and pitching an approach
- building and testing a working prototype
- documenting results, constraints, and recommendations for future development
